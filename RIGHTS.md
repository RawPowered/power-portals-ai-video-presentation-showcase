The project owner has explicitly confirmed that they own the rights to the PowerPortals presentation and its *Steady Progress* soundtrack, which they produced themselves. The owner authorizes public display of the rendered presentation videos **with that music embedded** and public display of selected project images.

Permission to watch or download a finished MP4 with synchronized soundtrack does **not** grant permission to separately extract, reuse, resell, remix, or redistribute the underlying music.

The public showcase is a portfolio presentation, not an open-source or Creative Commons release. No general license to reuse the project identity, graphics, videos, or music is granted.

## Publication boundaries

**Permitted:** Finished, reviewed PowerPortals presentation MP4s that contain synchronized owner-owned music; reviewed project screenshots, critique boards, and short visual excerpts.

**Not permitted:** Separate WAV/MP3/FLAC/M4A audio tracks or other standalone soundtrack exports, whether in source folders or as additional downloads.

**Also excluded:** Raw customer/account screenshots, personal contact details, authentication sessions, credentials, confidential operating records, unpublished internal system materials and third-party assets without applicable rights. Illustrative task assignees in critique 12 are fictional.

Automated repository checks aim to reject committed standalone audio formats, but reviewers still must inspect every proposed publication. A legal/brand license or technical performance certification is not implied.
