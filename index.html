04 / Feedback is a specification

Not “make it better.”
Make it testable.

The most transferable skill is turning subjective feedback into an observable change request and checking the actual result.

OBSERVE

Name the communication problem.

Reference the exact scene, object or event—not just a broad style preference.

CONSTRAIN

Define what changes and stays.

Set movement paths, reading holds, destinations, focal elements and proof requirements.

VERIFY

Review the rendered behavior.

Judge playback timing at speed and distinguish recorded captures from synthetic examples.

DOCUMENT

Keep trade-offs visible.

Explain why an alternative was retained, what improved and what evidence cannot establish.
