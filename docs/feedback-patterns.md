# Feedback patterns

These are reusable instructions distilled from the project, not additional historical quotes. Verbatim project feedback is separately preserved in the selected-feedback evidence.

## From “TV-friendly” to an actionable instruction

Use one dominant visual, free-standing type and one sequential idea. Preserve a readable hold; let the next animation carry the explanation.

[See the result](critiques/01.md)

## From “Smooth the transition” to an actionable instruction

Keep the same orange object. Define its destination position, final size and arrival time before choosing its path.

[See the result](critiques/10.md)

## From “Show the benefit” to an actionable instruction

Name the work problem, then show the action that addresses it. Keep the functional proof visible beneath the short headline.

[See the result](critiques/12.md)

## From “Make it applicable” to an actionable instruction

Keep the concrete named example in the close-up; generalize only when the diagram widens to a reusable relationship.

[See the result](critiques/11.md)

## From “Polish the type” to an actionable instruction

Use the shared alphabetic baseline and consistent column edges; do not judge alignment from the outer glyph box alone.

[See the result](critiques/08.md)

## From “Fix the audio” to an actionable instruction

Respect the listening feedback, identify the actual music source and keep effects on a separate timeline. Do not invent the cause of the impression.

[See the result](critiques/14.md)

## From “Shorten it” to an actionable instruction

Say where the time comes from and what attention or reading trade-off is being accepted. Preserve the prior version.

[See the result](critiques/15.md)
