# Evidence and attribution

## What is supported

The public final film is an exact copy of the selected archived export. The frame extracts come from real rendered versions at recorded timestamps. Annotation boards are retrospective overlays on those extracts; the underlying product pixels were not regenerated for the case study. The source hashes make those identities reviewable.

The selected feedback was transcribed from the project conversation. It is not a complete chat export, and no per-message timestamps are invented. The critique rationale was written after production. It is editorial analysis, not hidden model reasoning or a contemporaneous quote.

Historical production checks establish runtime, resolution, frame count, baseline/position consistency, source music identity and absence of clipping. The public packaging check establishes local links, hashes and the included asset list. Neither establishes sales uplift or audience comprehension.

## Product and demonstration limits

The BOM example uses 32 × 475.50 = 15,216.00 and a planned total of 43,757.25. The expanded organization graph shows configured relationship types. It is not proof that all roles or a particular Hillsborough-to-deal association were populated.

The people, assignments, progress-to-completion sequence and customer reply are illustrative/synthetic. The reply is an unsent draft. A historical automation report is not a newly filmed successful execution. The material does not establish an end-to-end bank or solar API sync.

The final API sentence is owner-directed marketing copy. The showcase documents the design process, not testing of every API, authentication method, CRM schema or deployment. Industry names describe adaptable workflows; they are not proof of a tested template for every trade.

The five-second small footer is an attention choice. Full read-through time is reduced; the complete wording is available by pausing the film. No legal-sufficiency or measured-comprehension claim is made.

## Public selection boundary

This repository is a newly assembled public selection, not a visibility change or history copy of the private archive. It includes the selected finished film, actual frame extracts, annotated boards, short silent motion excerpts and concise evidence. It omits raw browser captures, account details, internal production code snapshots, unrelated repositories, local machine paths, credentials and the standalone supplied song.

The full private stage archive retains original exports and internal source references. Public source identities are provided so the selected evidence can be checked independently. No live account access is needed to review the showcase.
