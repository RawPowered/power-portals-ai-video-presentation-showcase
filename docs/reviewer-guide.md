# A quick way to review

## Start with the outcome

Watch the [selected 1:54 film](../media/versions/31.mp4), then read the [case study](case-study.md).

## Inspect the judgement

Start with [composition](critiques/01.md), [direct slash](critiques/10.md), [benefit-led copy](critiques/12.md) and [the short ending](critiques/15.md). Each shows the actual states, the instruction and the reason the earlier treatment was superseded or kept as an alternative.

Use motion excerpts and phase strips for the transition cases, and the final film for the selected music and effects. The public selection does not include all 24 full historical exports; the ledger and extraction hashes preserve their identities without exposing the private archive.

## Ask useful questions

- Was a real communication problem identified?
- Was the instruction specific enough to act on?
- Does the artifact show the requested change?
- Are the trade-offs and evidence boundaries explained honestly?

The 17 cases are a portfolio of design decisions, not a claim of independently measured business results.
