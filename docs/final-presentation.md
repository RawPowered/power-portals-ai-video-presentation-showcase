# The selected final presentation

[Watch the 1:54 final film](../media/versions/31.mp4) · [Compare the early animatic](../media/versions/04.mp4)

## The story

The final cut turns a broad software walkthrough into a benefit-led presentation. It moves from one project’s competing demands into connected roles, task ownership, material costs, county relationships, automation and customer communication.

Large typography and a few words at a time carry the message. A recurring orange focal object connects the scenes, becoming a completion check, total, workflow arrow, reply surface and the PowerPortals brand slash.

## The creative decisions

- **Show responsibility:** People become assigned task owners, and progress reaches completion.
- **Make costs visible:** Quantity and price lead into the full bill of materials and its total.
- **Explain relationships:** A county entry expands into the organizational structure around deals, customers and projects.
- **Show cause and effect:** A trigger grows into branching actions, then transforms into the next scene.
- **Demonstrate service:** A customer message opens into a team reply, keeping the benefit concrete.
- **Broaden the audience:** Changing orange industry names extend the presentation beyond its solar example.

## The final polish

The selected cut runs 114 seconds at 1080p60. The owner-produced *Steady Progress* track supports separately placed sound effects. Typography alignment, reading holds and direct object-to-object transitions keep attention on the message.

A five-second closing footer replaces the longer disclosure sequence. This keeps the brand central and shortens the ending, with less time for a full disclosure read-through. That trade-off is documented in [critique 15](critiques/15.md).

The early 28-second introduction remains as a comparison. The [17 critique studies](critique-index.md) explain how the composition, motion, messaging and sound direction evolved.
