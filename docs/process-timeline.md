## The runtime was a design variable

The broad initial walkthrough targeted 165 seconds. The commercial explorations used 28- and 40-second versions. Real interactions needed more time, leading to 60 seconds. Ownership, county relationships and service detail grew the cut to 72, 93 and 108 seconds. Focused retiming reduced 108 to 103.25 seconds. Benefit copy, industry flips and extended disclosure created a 134-second V2. A brief final hold accommodated the supplied song at 134.56 seconds; the selected five-second-footer close brought it to 114 seconds.

No runtime is presented as inherently superior. The decision depends on proof, reading time and the intended viewing context.

## Capture and rendering evidence

The capture report records 17 live takes, with 12 editing candidates and five diagnostics/duplicates. Browser source resolution was 1280×720 or 1262×884 at a 30fps export; later 1080p60 presentation graphics do not convert that source into native 1080p footage.

The capture phase recorded an unpublished preview-page draft and a language preference change. BOM and reply input remained unsaved drafts. Later animation revisions were local production work. The public repo includes the presentation selection rather than raw captures or account state.
